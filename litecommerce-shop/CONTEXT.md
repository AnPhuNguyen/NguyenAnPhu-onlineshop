# Shop Context

Glossary for the LiteCommerce Shop frontend.

## Glossary

### Customer
A shopper who interacts with the system through the Shop portal to browse products and place orders.

### Cart
A session-based temporary storage for products before an Order is created. It is cleared upon logout or session expiration and is not synchronized across different devices/sessions.

### Province
A fixed geographical area for delivery, selected during checkout.

### Cover Photo
The image displayed in product lists and at the top of the detail page.

### Product Attribute
Detailed specifications (like Size or Material) shown on the product detail page.

### Price Filter
A navigational filter typically presented as predefined price "buckets" (ranges).

### Availability
A product is considered available for purchase only if `IsSelling` is set to `1` (true).

### Account
The authentication identity used by a Customer (Email and Password).
